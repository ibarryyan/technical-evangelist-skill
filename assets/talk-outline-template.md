# Talk / Deck Outline

## Positioning

```
Title:
Audience:                    (who is in the room, and what they already know)
Slot length:
Deck objective:              (the one thing the room must remember)
Thesis — one sentence:
  The core idea is: ______________________________________________
Call to action:              (what they do in the next 24 hours)
Tier:                        (conference / meetup / internal / customer)
```

## First five minutes

```
1. Why they should care:      <in their terms>
2. What problem gets solved:  <specific, demonstrable>
3. What they will be able to do afterwards:  <concrete capability>
```

## Storyline

| # | Section | Min | Key message | Slide(s) | Show |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening | 2–3 | Why care / problem / payoff | 2–3 | Hook image or failing state |
| 2 | Problem and context | 4–5 | The current approach breaks like this | 3–4 | Evidence, number |
| 3 | Insight | 3 | The thesis | 1 | The thesis, stated on screen |
| 4 | Technical explanation | 9 | How it works | 6–8 | Architecture diagram |
| 5 | Demo | 6 | It works, and here is the value | — | Live, with fallback |
| 6 | Lessons and trade-offs | 3–4 | Where it bites | 2–3 | Failure story |
| 7 | Close | 1–2 | Takeaway + action | 1 | Takeaway, not "Questions?" |

Total planned: <85% of slot>
